# Unresolved Operations Policies — Do Not Fabricate

1. **Country and seller-of-record** for tax/invoicing, qualified invoice registration and payment gateways; Japan is used in JPY examples but legal launch country not confirmed.
2. **Inbound vendor → warehouse carrier charge:** vendor or platform; upfront transparency and disputes.
3. **Courier COD:** which carriers legally/contractually remit to marketplace, whether vendor self-fulfilled COD can use marketplace collection, and bank settlement windows.
4. **Buyer shipping charged separately?** Examples assumed product totals only. Define billing, tax, promotions and how customer shipping amounts flow into ledger.
5. **Shipping cost allocation:** per shipment actual cost vs rate card; multi-vendor shipment allocation methodology, overrides and rounding.
6. **Payout:** frequency, minimum threshold, return-risk hold, negative balance and refunds after payout.
7. **Refund and delivery fault:** responsibility for failed delivery, customer absence, wrong address, damaged goods, return-to-origin, exchanges.
8. **Promotions:** vendor self-publish vs admin approve, minimum price restrictions, discount stacking policies.
9. **Locale:** default, fallback, translated content review policy, target market, address formats, regulatory notice wording.
10. **Recommendation privacy:** consent, event retention and analytics purpose; vendor report granularity.
